Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026

The short version. Nook keeps what you put on your boards so you and the people you share them with can see it. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. You can delete everything from inside the app.

What Nook stores

  • Your account — your email address and a password. The password is stored as a one-way hash by our sign-in provider; nobody can read it.
  • Your boards — their names and everything on them: items, quantities, notes and drawings.
  • Voice memos — the recordings you make, stored privately and playable only by people on that board.
  • Who is on each board — the email addresses of people who joined it, and when.

Nook also keeps a copy of your boards in your browser so it works with no signal. Signing out clears that copy.

Who can see it

Only you, and the people you have shared a board with by its link. Remove someone and they lose access; make a new link and the old one stops working. Anyone on a board can edit it; only the person who made it can delete it.

Services Nook relies on

  • Supabase stores accounts, boards and voice memos, and sends sign-in and password emails.
  • Vercel hosts the website and app. Like any web host it sees your IP address and keeps short-lived request logs.
  • Iconify supplies the little product pictures. To find one, the name of an item (for example “oat milk”) is sent to Iconify’s public icon service — nothing else about you or your board is.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the account menu and choose Delete account. That permanently removes your account, every board you made (for everyone on it) and their voice memos. Boards other people shared with you stay with them; your place on them is removed. To leave a single board instead, press and hold it (or right-click) and choose Leave board.

Children

Nook is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly hold their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it.
